# inbox/

Drop raw material here. The weekly drafting run reads everything in this folder.

> ⚠️ **This repo is public.** Anything you put here can be seen by anyone. Only upload material you'd be
> happy to post: crop notifications, emails, other apps and personal data out of screenshots and
> recordings. Your weekly notes (what you shipped, broke, learned) go in `notes/weekly.md` in the private repo
> `content-engine-private`, not here.

| What | How | Becomes |
|---|---|---|
| Screenshots (`.png`/`.jpg`) | App screens, analytics, the widget on your home screen. | Image slides in carousels. |
| Screen recordings (`.mp4`) | 10–40 s of the app in use. Landscape or portrait both work. | Faceless Reels/Shorts with auto-captions. |
| Talking-head clips (`.mp4`) | Record the script from the PR, save with the exact file name it asks for. | Captioned Reels/Shorts. |

**Uploading from your phone:** github.com → this repo → `inbox` → *Add file* → *Upload files*.
The web upload limit is 25 MB — a 30–45 s clip at 1080p usually fits; if not, shrink it with any
free video-compressor app first. Larger files can be pushed from your laptop with git (up to 100 MB).

Recording tips: phone vertical, eye level, window light in front of you, quiet room, first sentence = the hook.
