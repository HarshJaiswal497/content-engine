Everyone likes ugly theme. My first set of themes was pretty ugly. I'd only swapped the colours on the same screen, no layout change, nothing. So I started over, and made each theme a real thing. A fridge door, with magnets and a breeze. A record sleeve, with the record sliding out. An old book's page, with a wax seal. Now there are 8 themes in my app and everyone picks their own in every circle. Slipnote is in development, and the waitlist is in my bio. Thank you.
