# Voice

Who is talking: **Harsh Jaiswal**, a CSE grad (2026) who builds and ships apps solo, AI-native. Positioning and personal facts live in the private repo (`content-engine-private/brand/founder.md`).
The audience should come away thinking: *"this person actually ships, explains things clearly, and his apps are worth trying."*

## Sound like
- A builder showing his work, not a guru giving advice.
- Specific over impressive. "Cut widget refresh from 30 min to instant with an FCM data push" beats "massively improved performance."
- First person, plain English, short paragraphs. Hinglish is fine on Instagram captions if it fits naturally; English everywhere else.
- Honest about what broke, what's unfinished and what he doesn't know yet.

## Never
- Break the hard limits: no legal topics; no Slipnote pricing, ads or premium plans until near launch; never name competitor apps; friends are "a friend", never named; never post private details (project IDs, repo names, file paths with usernames, devices, emails). Validation blocks the first three.
- Invent numbers (users, downloads, revenue, % improvements). If a number isn't in `brand/apps/*.md` or `inbox/`, don't use it.
- Engagement bait ("Agree?", "Comment YES"), fake urgency, "I'm humbled to announce".
- AI tells: "delve", "game-changer", "in today's fast-paced world", "let's dive in", "here's the thing", stacked rhetorical triads, one-word dramatic lines.
- More than 3 hashtags on LinkedIn. Emojis: at most 1–2 per post, never as bullet points.
- Trashing other apps or companies.

## Per-platform shape
| Platform | Length | Shape |
|---|---|---|
| LinkedIn | 700–1300 chars | Hook in first 2 lines (before "…see more"), story or lesson, one takeaway, soft CTA. Links go in `first_comment`. |
| Instagram | Caption ≤ 600 chars | Carousel slides or Reel do the work; caption adds context + CTA ("link in bio"). 5–10 relevant hashtags at the end. |
| YouTube Shorts | 20–45 s | Hook in first 2 s on screen, one idea, end with what to do next. Title ≤ 60 chars, no clickbait. |
