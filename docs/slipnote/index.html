Three ways to slip a note

Everything is drawn by hand. There's no typed text, on purpose. A stabiliser keeps your handwriting neat.

Handwritten notes

Write on paper styles with a pen, marker, pencil or highlighter. Undo, zoom, and an eraser that never touches the paper.

Doodles

Draw on a colour, add stickers, and send. It lands on their widget within seconds.

Photos you draw on

Snap a photo or pick one from your gallery, scribble on top, and slip it onto their screen.

A widget for every circle

Your partner, your friend group, your family: one widget each, in small, medium or large. Tap a note to open it, tap the pen to doodle back.

  • Your widget updates even when the app is closed.
  • Nothing ever covers a friend's note. No streaks to keep alive, no guilt.
  • The app never deletes your notes.
  • Pairs or groups, and as many circles as you like.
